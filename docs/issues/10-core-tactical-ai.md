## What happens today
- `BattleAI` plays by synthesising `QGraphicsSceneMouseEvent`s on frames (`AI/battleai.cpp:runAnimation`, `applyMoves` call `mousePressEventAI()`) and busy-waits with `QCoreApplication::processEvents()` until animations finish — the UI freezes for the AI's whole turn and the AI cannot run without a scene.
- Decisions are a flat rating list (`movesRating`: escape a threatened general 10000, strike a general 9000, otherwise unit cost) with no look-ahead (`battleai.cpp:detectThretenedUnits`…`detectForkPossibilities`), so it walks into forks and mates that a one-ply search sees.

## Change

- Draw/surrender rules (§4.4).
