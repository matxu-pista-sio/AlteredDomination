# [Core] AI-vs-AI auto-resolve through the real board engine, with a performance budget for a full AI round

Labels: enhancement · Type: Feature

## Reported
> improve the Ai layout and logic

## What happens today
`MapAI::startBattleAI_AI` (`AI/mapai.cpp:427-500`) resolves a battle between two AI countries with one weighted coin flip (`qrand() % (x + y)`) and then deletes a random number of random units on each side — unit types, formations and generals play no part in most of the game's battles.

## Change
- `autoResolve(catalog, attackerUnits, defenderUnits, budget) → BattleResult` runs the tactical AI (#10) on both sides at the `Quick` budget through the real `Battle` engine (#8), honouring every rule including the turn caps ([docs/AI_DESIGN.md](https://github.com/matxu-pista-sio/AlteredDomination/blob/claude/dreamy-volta-pvb4kh/docs/AI_DESIGN.md) §3).
- `AiRound` (#9) uses it for every AI-vs-AI battle; a setup option lets the human auto-resolve their own battles at `Normal`.
- Performance: a full AI round on the generated world is measured in `test_ai_round.cpp` by total node count (bound asserted) and reported in wall time; target < 2 s release on a laptop core. If the bound is missed, the Quick budget or the round's battle count is tuned here, not in the callers.

## Acceptance criteria
- Two identical armies auto-resolve deterministically for a given seed; an army with fighters against an army without anti-aircraft wins most of a 20-sample set (a real rule, not a coin).
- Round-time bound asserted; a `--benchmark` doctest subcase prints the timing.
- Suggested commits: `#11 core: autoResolve`, `#11 core: AiRound uses it`, `#11 core: benchmark test`.
