## What happens today
- The battle rules are `BFrame::mousePressEvent` (`graphics/bframe.cpp:60-330`): a 270-line switch over phases that also does the input handling, the multiplayer sends and the animations. Win detection is `BFrame::checkWineLose` reading `attackerMoves == 0`, where `attackerMoves` is both the general count and the per-turn move budget (`domain/battlemap.cpp:19`).
- Board width is `1 + max(nA, nD) / 2` (`battlemap.cpp:16`), which yields a 1-row board for tiny fights and 51 rows for 100 units.
- Legal moves/strikes are computed three times over (`bframe.cpp:highlightMovesAttacks`, `battleai.cpp:attackListForFrame`, `moveListForFrame`) with subtly different rules.

## Change
`core/include/ad/core/battle.hpp` per [docs/GAME_DESIGN.md](https://github.com/matxu-pista-sio/AlteredDomination/blob/claude/dreamy-volta-pvb4kh/docs/GAME_DESIGN.md) §8:
- `Battle(catalog, attackerUnits, defenderUnits)`: 14 × `w` board (`w = clamp(5, 2 + ceil(max/3), 16)`), engine formation (§8.3), phases `Deploy → Promote → Play → Over`.
- Commands: `Rearrange`, `Ready`, `Promote`, `Demote`, `MoveUnit`, `Strike`, `EndTurn`, `OfferDraw`, `Surrender`; every one validated and answered with a result enum.
- One legal-action generator (`legalMoves(cell)`, `legalStrikes(cell)`, `legalActions(side)`) used by the UI highlights, the AI and the tests; defender patterns mirrored on `dx`.
- Generals (`1 + n/8`, soldiers only with conversion), actions per turn = living generals, one action per unit per turn, auto-pass, generalmate, surrender, draw offers, turn cap 80 / quiet cap 30; `result()` with survivors; `stateHash()`.
