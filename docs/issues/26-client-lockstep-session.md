## What happens today
`GameController` owns one local campaign; every human seat is played at this table.

## Change
- `GameController::startOnline(frame)`: the campaign from the server's seed, terms and seats; `me()` is the local seat; `humanTurn` / `remoteTurn`; every applied command is sent (`sendNet`); relayed frames go through an **inbox** drained only when the campaign can take the next one (between turns - never over an AI round or a board).
- Battles: the deciding seat answers the prompt and relays `battle_choice`; the board is played on the deciding client (its AI moves relayed as `bcmd`) or by both humans; `BattleController` sides are human / remote / AI, with `applyRemote`, `commandApplied`, `remoteRetreat`.
- Round hashes (`sync`) after every AI round; results reported on domination and elimination; `resign()`.
- Top bar: the opponent and "X is playing…"; the menu: Resign instead of Save; the prompt waits for the deciding seat; the overlay shows the verdict.
