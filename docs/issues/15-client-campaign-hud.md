## What happens today
- `CityUI.qml` (550 lines, absolute layout) is a floating window; `AddUnitUI.qml` mutates `country.funds` from QML (`AddUnitUI.qml:195`); `MoveUnitsUI.qml` moves units by emitting signals per unit id; `AttackUI.qml` shows both armies and one button.
- End turn runs the whole AI on the UI thread inside `MainMenu::runNextTurn`; the window freezes for seconds with no feedback; AI battles against the player pop the battle window mid-freeze.
- Ranking is rebuilt from scratch by emitting one signal per country (`mainmenu.cpp:reDisplayCountries`).

## Change
`client/qml/Campaign.qml` composed of `components/`:
- **Top bar**: flag + country, round, funds, income (+ delta), world share gauge toward the domination threshold, End turn (E), menu.
- **City sheet** (`GlassPanel`, slides in on selection): name, tier, owner, income, units grouped by type with icons; **Recruit** (type tiles with cost, quantity, running total against funds); **Move** and **Attack** modes highlight targets on the map, the target click opens the force picker (which units go, defaults to all unacted); attack on an undefended city captures with a flash.
- **Ranking** panel (`CountryModel`), sortable, the human row pinned.
- **End turn**: the AI round runs on a worker; a slim progress strip shows "Japan is moving…", captures fly a toast and the map recolours as they happen; when an AI attacks the player a prompt offers *Fight* / *Auto-resolve* and the battle page pushes.
- **Game over** overlay (victory / defeat) with the final ranking and *Back to menu*.
- Autosave at round end; `Escape` opens the in-game menu (Save, Settings, Quit to menu with confirmation).
