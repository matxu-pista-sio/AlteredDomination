## What happens today
- Ownership colouring rewrites the map SVG on disk (`graphics/mapgraphics.cpp:reavaluteCountrties` writes `applicationDirPath()/out.svg` and re-parses the 600 KB file every turn) and colours a **whole country** by the owner of the majority of its income — a city conquered in the middle of Russia does not show.
- 9 countries of the JSON have no path in `out.svg` and 20 paths belong to no country (cross-check of ids vs `flag` fields).

## Change
Extend `scripts/gen_world_data.py` ([docs/DATA_PIPELINE.md](https://github.com/matxu-pista-sio/AlteredDomination/blob/claude/dreamy-volta-pvb4kh/docs/DATA_PIPELINE.md) §6):
- **Territory per city**: Voronoi cell of the city over all cities, intersected with its country polygon; islands without a city fall to the nearest city of that country; simplified with a 0.8-unit tolerance; written as SVG path strings (multiple subpaths for multipolygons) to `assets/world/territories.json` keyed by city key.
- **Outlines**: one simplified path per country to `assets/world/outlines.json`.
- **Banner colours**: 48-hue table, greedy assignment so no two countries whose territories touch share a hue; stored on the country in `world.json`.
- Every coordinate in map units (§3) so the client feeds the strings straight into `PathSvg` (the map view of #14 renders them with the `QtQuick.Shapes` curve renderer and recolours a territory with a `ColorAnimation` on conquest).

## Acceptance criteria

- No adjacent countries share a banner hue (checker).
- `territories.json` + `outlines.json` together ≤ 4 MB.
