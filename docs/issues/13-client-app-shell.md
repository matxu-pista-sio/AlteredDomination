## What happens today
- `HomeMenu` is a `QQuickView`; the game is a `QWidget` (`MainMenu`, `mainmenu.ui`) hosting a `QGraphicsView` and two `QQuickWidget`s; the city sheet, recruit, move, attack and options panels are five more frameless `QQuickView` windows with `Qt::ToolTip` flags (`graphics/mapview.cpp:60-140`) that each `setEnabled(false)` the map while open. Focus and z-order break on every desktop environment.
- The intro (`scripts/home/Intro.qml`) cannot be skipped; the home screen animates 18 PNG frames of a rotating earth.
- The new-game page is a `ComboBox` of 174 names with no search, flag or info.

## Change
- `client/src/main.cpp`: `QGuiApplication`, high-DPI pass-through, style + fonts, `QQmlApplicationEngine::loadFromModule("AD", "Main")`, `--profile`, `AD_DRIVE` hook (#19).
