## What happens today
The rules live in the graphics classes: capture in `graphics/citygraphics.cpp:startBattle` (230-275), the whole end-of-turn in `mainmenu.cpp:runNextTurn` (126-165, which also pays income *after* the AI has attacked and resets every city's `used` flag globally), win detection nowhere on the map (a human can never actually win). Movement marks the whole **city** as used (`citygraphics.cpp:moveUnits`), so moving one unit forbids attacking with the rest. `qrand()` is seeded from the clock in three files.

## Change
`core/include/ad/core/{campaign,commands}.hpp`, normative rules in [docs/GAME_DESIGN.md](https://github.com/matxu-pista-sio/AlteredDomination/blob/claude/dreamy-volta-pvb4kh/docs/GAME_DESIGN.md) §3–§7, §9:
- `CampaignSettings{seed, mode (Gdp|Equality), difficulty, humanCountries[]}`; `Campaign(World&, Catalog&, settings)` seeds every country with `kStartingRounds` of income, one player per country, AI turn order shuffled once with the seed.
- Income formula (§3) with the GDP exponent and the capital bonus; difficulty scale on AI income; capital loot.
- Commands with validation results: `Recruit`, `Move`, `Attack`, `EndTurn`; per-unit `acted` flags cleared at the owner's turn start.
- `Attack` on an undefended city captures at once; on a defended one returns `NeedsBattle{attackerUnits, defenderUnits}` and `resolveBattle(BattleResult)` applies the outcome (§5.3), including the 48-per-side cap.
- Round bookkeeping: elimination, domination victory (§9), `GameOver` state, ranking query, `stateHash()`.
- Hotseat: several human countries take turns before the AIs (§7).
