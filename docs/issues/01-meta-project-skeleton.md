## What happens today
- `AlteredDomination.pro` is a qmake project for Qt 5 (`QT += widgets quickwidgets svg xml multimedia network`, `CONFIG += resources_big`). It cannot build against Qt 6: `QtGraphicalEffects 1.0` and `QtQuick.Controls 1.6` are gone, `qrand`/`QSound`/`QGraphicsSvgItem` were removed or moved.
- Domain, graphics, AI, UI and networking are one flat target (`.pro` `SOURCES`); nothing can be tested without a display.
- 225 MB of `data/` are embedded as resources, including a saved Jira page (`data/icons/AD board - Agile Board - AFKAAR_files/`), three identical 4.4 MB map SVGs, a 14 MB `mapthem.svg`, a 2.3 MB `.bmp` logo and 100 MB of sounds duplicated as wav + mp3.
- No README, license, CI, tests, `.clang-format` or `.editorconfig`.

Full audit: [docs/MODERNIZATION_PLAN.md](https://github.com/matxu-pista-sio/AlteredDomination/blob/claude/dreamy-volta-pvb4kh/docs/MODERNIZATION_PLAN.md) §2.1.
